Guardrails

It won’t put words in your mouth.

If a rewrite looks wrong in any of these ways, it’s thrown away and your raw transcript is pasted instead. One- and two-word dictations are never rewritten.

  • changes a number
  • grows past 1.6×
  • shrinks below 0.5×
  • keeps <60% of your words
  • answers instead of fixing
  • adds paragraphs
  • translates
  • repeats the last dictation

Under the hood

From button to cursor in seven steps.

One dictation is processed at a time, so quick ones queue instead of fighting over the GPU. The log keeps what was heard next to what was pasted.

  1. Record 16 kHz

    Hold the shortcut. On Windows it’s swallowed, so the side button stops paging your browser back.

  2. Gate < 0.5 s dropped

    Silence is trimmed, quiet speech boosted, and clips with no speech (a fan, a knock) are dropped.

  3. Transcribe twice ~1 s

    Qwen3-ASR-1.7B, with and without your accent note, in one batched call.

  4. Clean up instant

    “one two three” becomes 123, “comma” becomes a comma, filler words go. Then corrections.txt.

  5. Fix the English ~1 s

    The local model gets both hearings plus your recent dictations as context.

  6. Check instant

    Guardrails compare the rewrite with the transcript. When in doubt, the transcript wins.

  7. Paste Ctrl/Cmd+V

    Into whatever window has focus. The text stays on the clipboard too.

Questions

The honest fine print.

Does anything get sent to the internet?

It depends on the engine you pick. The local engine (Qwen3-ASR) never touches the network: models are downloaded once from Hugging Face, then the app runs with HF_HUB_OFFLINE=1. The Gemini engine uploads the clip to Google with your own API key; the optional Claude engine uses your own Claude login. The English fixer is local in every case. There is no telemetry, and no account with us.

Is it tested on macOS and Linux?

Not yet on real hardware. It was built and measured on Windows 11 (Core Ultra 5 228V). The macOS and Linux code paths are written and share the same core. On Linux you need an X11 session, because Wayland blocks global shortcuts and tray icons for ordinary apps.

Which languages work?

Speech recognition covers 52 languages and dialects, including strong Chinese and mixed Chinese-English. The rewrite step is for English only, so turn off Fix my English in the tray if you dictate in another language.

What if the fixer changes my meaning?

The guardrails reject most bad rewrites and paste your raw words instead. The log keeps what was heard next to what was pasted, so you can add repeat mistakes to corrections.txt, or switch the fixer off with one click.

Can I use Gemma 4 instead of Qwen?

Yes, from the tray. Gemma 4 E2B scored 28/28 but needs transformers 5, so it runs as a worker in a second environment (.venv-t5). If it fails to load, the app falls back to Qwen3-1.7B and says why in the log.

Why doesn’t it type into some windows?

Windows won’t let a normal app send keystrokes to one running as administrator. Use Restart as Administrator from the tray for those.
